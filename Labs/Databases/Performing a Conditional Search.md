## Lab Overview

In this lab, I worked with a relational database to perform conditional searches using SQL. The focus was on applying the **SELECT** statement together with the **WHERE** clause to filter and retrieve specific records from the dataset.
I explored the use of operators such as BETWEEN and LIKE, along with functions and column aliases, to refine queries and extract meaningful insights based on defined conditions.

### Steps:

   1. I queried the world database using SQL SELECT statements with conditional filtering to retrieve specific subsets of data. I applied comparison operators and logical conditions to limit the result set and make the data more focused and manageable.
      I also used the **BETWEEN** operator for range-based queries and the **LIKE** operator with wildcard characters to search for patterns within text. Additionally, I applied functions such as **SUM** and **LOWER**, along with column aliases, to perform calculations and improve the readability of query results.
